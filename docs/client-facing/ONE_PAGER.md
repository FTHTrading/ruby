# Allure Ruby & Siam Emerald RWA — Executive Summary

**Institutional real-world asset tokenization** | FTHTrading / Troptions ecosystem | *One page — non-binding overview*

---

## The opportunity

Museum-grade colored gemstones rarely trade with the transparency, auditability, and operational efficiency that institutional capital expects. Owners and family offices need to **monetize or collateralize** exceptional physical assets without unnecessary fire sales, while investors need **verifiable provenance**, regulated access, and programmable lifecycle mechanics.

This program structures a **54.00 carat heated ruby** (Allure Ruby — multi-laboratory gemological certification) together with a **complementary polished emerald** (Siam / global trading-hub context) as a coordinated RWA package. **Target package value is subject to independent third-party appraisal**; no NAV is asserted in this document.

---

## The problem

| Challenge | Why it matters |
|-----------|----------------|
| Opaque custody and title history | Institutions cannot underwrite without auditable chain-of-custody |
| Sensitive documentation | Full lab reports and ownership records cannot sit on public ledgers |
| Slow, manual investor operations | High-touch assets need scalable KYC, reporting, and proof delivery |
| Disconnected payment rails | Token issuance alone does not settle distributions, collateral events, or onboarding flows |

---

## Our solution

A **sovereign RWA operating stack** that separates **private provenance** from **public issuance**, with automation for client operations and ecosystem settlement:

| Layer | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Legacy Vault + Legacy Chain** | Encrypted document storage, manifests, and **permissioned on-chain anchoring** for SPV events, custody, and VC digests — not public Solana |
| **Troptions / troptionsmint.com** | **Solana Token-2022** institutional mint (compliance extensions, authority revocation, metadata URIs to public summaries) |
| **GemAssetCredential (VCDM 2.0)** | W3C-aligned verifiable credentials; **SD-JWT default issuance**, **BBS+** for high-privacy guardian presentations |
| **Agent Mailor** | AI client-operations layer: intake, KYC/AML coordination, secure comms, status and proof packages |
| **x402 (ecosystem)** | Programmable Web3 **settlement rail** for payments tied to asset lifecycle events (where deployed in the Troptions stack) |
| **GMIIE / xxxiii.io** | Market intelligence and oracle references — **does not replace independent appraisal** |

---

## Four-phase client journey

1. **Secure custody & provenance** — Physical asset verification, encrypted vault package, Legacy Chain anchors, Gem VC issuance via Legacy Vault only.
2. **SPV & token design** — Legal entity, accredited-investor framework (as applicable), Token-2022 specification and metadata policy (no NAV in metadata until appraisal complete).
3. **Onboarding & settlement** — troptionsmint issuance path; Agent Mailor for investor workflows; x402-enabled programmable settlement where available.
4. **Liquidity & governance** — Secondary market participation, selective disclosure to counterparties, multi-proof release engine for high-assurance actions.

---

## Institutional benefits

- **Sovereign provenance** — Cryptographic audit trail for title, custody, and material events without exposing sensitive files on a public chain.
- **Institutional liquidity path** — Tokenized representation and Troptions rails designed for private placement and compliant transfer policies.
- **Privacy with compliance** — Prove only what each counterparty requires (lending, secondary market, regulatory, guardian).
- **Operational scale** — Agent Mailor reduces manual overhead for reporting, intake, and investor communications.
- **Standards-based** — W3C DID + VCDM 2.0, Solana Token-2022; private infrastructure retained where appropriate.
