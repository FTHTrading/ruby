## How it works

1. **Legal & custody** — SPV holds economic interest; physical assets in secure custody with documented chain of custody.  
2. **Legacy Vault** — Encrypted lab reports, manifests, and verifiable credentials (DID/VC).  
3. **Token issuance** — Solana **Token-2022** with compliance extensions: metadata pointer, default-frozen accounts, required transfer memos, and a transfer hook for policy enforcement (program deployment TBD).  
4. **Distribution** — Private placement workflows; thaw and transfer rules enforced by hook + credential checks.  
5. **Market intelligence** — GMIIE oracle references for comps narrative only (not a substitute for appraisal).

---

## Token-2022 compliance rails (summary)

Tokens are designed as **Token-2022** assets so transfers can carry mandatory audit memos and pass through a **transfer hook** that consults Legacy Vault verifiable credentials (including optional BBS+ selective disclosure) before settlement. New holder accounts may start **frozen** until compliance approval. Public on-chain metadata links only to **URI-based** disclosure packages — never to raw certificate PDFs. Minting and authority revocation use the existing **troptionsmint.com** institutional console.
