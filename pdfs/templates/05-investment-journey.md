# Four-Phase Client Journey

## Phase 1 — Intake & verification

KYC/AML, encrypted vault upload, appraisal engagement (claims TBD).

## Phase 2 — Legal & custody

SPV, custody agreements, release policy in vault namespace.

## Phase 3 — Credentials & mint

VC issuance, sandbox Token-2022 mint, authority revocation policy.

## Phase 4 — Distribution & liquidity

Private placement documentation, secondary policy, ongoing audit on Legacy Chain.

---

Each phase gates the next. Mainnet promotion requires counsel sign-off and cleared appraisal references where economics are marketed.
