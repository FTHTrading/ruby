## Phase fees (indicative USD)

| Phase | Range | Outcome |
|-------|-------|---------|
| **0 — Readiness** | $100k – $250k | Architecture, VC v1, x402 spec, portal v1, client materials |
| **1 — Issuance-ready** | $200k – $500k | Legacy issuance, BBS+ prod, sandbox mint, intake |
| **2 — Production & raise** | $300k – $800k+ | Mainnet, settlement gateway, investor operations |
| **Annual retainer** | $180k – $600k/yr | Ops, schema updates, monitoring |

**Reference program (Phase 0–1):** $400,000 – $900,000

## Why not cheaper alternatives?

| Alternative | Typical cost | Gap |
|-------------|--------------|-----|
| Agency + generic token | $30k – $150k | No BBS+, vault, hooks, settlement |
| Tokenization SaaS | $50k – $300k | No custom gem VC, permissioned registry |
| Deck-only consulting | $200k – $800k | No working integrations or portal |

## What fees buy (trust mechanics)

- **Credentials** — prove lien/appraisal state without leaking full certs  
- **Vault + chain** — auditable custody separate from public Solana  
- **Token-2022** — compliance hooks + immutable supply narrative  
- **x402** — metered lifecycle settlement  
- **Portal + PDFs** — institutional first impression  

Appraisal, securities counsel, custody transfer, and mainnet keys are **excluded** unless Phase 2 SOW.
